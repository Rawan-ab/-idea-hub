# Idea Hub

## Private Shared App — Gift Release

A private mobile app for two people, accessible by invitation, designed as a shared personal space.

### Core ideas
- Shared photos and memories.
- Notes/messages presented as personal cards.
- Restaurants wishlist, visited status, and ratings from both people.
- Movies and series: want to watch, watching, watched, and ratings.
- Music and shared saved songs.
- Clothes / wishlist items.
- Notifications when the other person adds something.
- Invite-only access.
- Memories timeline.
- Share links directly from other apps; automatically classify links by content type (restaurant, music, movie, clothes/product, photo/video, or saved link).

### Design direction
Warm minimal visual language with soft rounded cards, cream/light backgrounds, translucent gradients, library/folder organization, playful message cards, modular notes supporting text/images/audio, and Polaroid/scrapbook-inspired memories.

### Goal
Build the first working Flutter mobile app as a personal gift before January 1, 2027.
